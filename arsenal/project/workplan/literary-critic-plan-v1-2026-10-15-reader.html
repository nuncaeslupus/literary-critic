▸Technical solution

▸Architecture overview

A plan layer sits above rounds. Rounds keep owning actions, and Codex decisions stay the record of each issue.

  1. src/litcritic/plan.py owns <ws>/plan/log.jsonl (append-only, the source of truth) and plan.json (its replay). It holds a pure transition function apply(state, event) -> state and the refusal rules. Every command appends one event, replays, and writes the snapshot atomically.
  2. lc plan (cli.py subcommands): init, add, accept, start, skip, done, reopen, move, edit, next, status and check. status joins the plan events, the change events, rounds.status and Codex decisions into one timeline.
  3. rounds.build gains area and issues filters and nothing else, so a writer-added aspect part can become a round on just that aspect.
  4. src/litcritic/change.py and lc change (propose, approve, dismiss, status). Proposals are stored as change.proposed events in the same log, with ids C1…. approve rebuilds the edit from the stored proposal only, and writes through apply_edits, which is extracted from docx_cli._edits. That is the same path lc docx edits uses, and it stays tracked until T8.
  5. MCP (mcp.py): the plan tool, the change_card tool with its page ui://literary-critic/change, and the app-only change_approve and change_dismiss. state() and next_step read the plan. RUN_ALLOWED gains plan and change.
  6. Prompts tell the assistant when to propose a part or a change, that a writer move needs the writer's word and reason, and how each surface shows the buttons. Prose stays behind prose_suggestions (house rule 5).
  7. The switch to plain writes (T8, after doc-control#318) sets "tracked": False and puts in the minimum-version check, the marked reason and the wording changes the spec lists. The live engines follow in T9 (after doc-control#319).

▸Out of scope

  • Any change to how rounds number, score or reuse actions, or to the Codex item types.
  • Edits longer than one paragraph, moves of text, or style changes through cards.
  • Comments in the manuscript (the existing comment paths stay as they are).
  • Adopting history after a move (doc-control#320); it is only cited.
  • A plan editor page. The plan is steered in the chat; plan status is what the writer sees.

▸Task details

  • T1 Plan log, replay and lc plan: the event schema and the pure apply; the commands and refusals from spec §5; init --mode seeds the parts for iterative or report mode, from the profile's mode. The log is appended first, then the snapshot is written to a temp file that replaces the old one; check and check --repair. plan joins workspace.SUBDIRS.
  • T2 Area and issue filters on round build: build(..., area=None, issues=None) filters the open problem issues before the level is chosen, and the CLI gets --area and --issues. Nothing else about rounds changes.
  • T3 Plan status, next and legacy seeding: the read-only join (plan events, change events, rounds.status, Codex decisions) and the timeline; next maps a part kind to its command. init on a workspace with rounds seeds one round part per round. With no plan/, status answers no_plan with the proposal.
  • T4 Change propose, approve and dismiss (tracked): the card in the work's language; the prose rule read from the profile; the size limits (12 changed words under minor, 1 paragraph under on); the resolve-once and one-paragraph checks; the repeat check, with --again. apply_edits is extracted from _edits, and approve sets the edit id to the change id; --closes marks the round action done. With edit_in_place = no or a manuscript that is not .docx, approve logs typed_by: writer and writes nothing. Writes stay tracked here.
  • T5 Undo marks a change undone: lc docx undo looks up the edit id it took back and logs change.undone; its answer gains "change".
  • T6 MCP plan tool and change card: the plan tool; change_card with its page; the app-only change_approve (which also runs docx locate --go) and change_dismiss. The page sends ui/message only when the host advertises it. state() and next_step read the plan, and RUN_ALLOWED gains plan and change.
  • T7 Prompts for the plan and the cards: when to propose parts (by tool) and when a move is the writer's; the order to follow in iterative and report mode; the cards on each surface (desktop page, widget buttons that send "Approve C1" / "Dismiss C1", text yes/no). The cards say "Word marks it" until T9.
  • T8 Q-1 probe of ui/message: the owner clicks Approve on a test card in the desktop app with a public-domain text from corpus/; record whether the model sees the message. The spec's Q-1 is updated.
  • T9 Plain writes in the file engine: once doc-control#318 is released, pin its minimum version in dc.py. Send "tracked": False in docx_cli (edits and rename); map UNSUPPORTED_CONTENT to marked; reword the docx_cli help, setup.py --edit-in-place help, word_live.py, prompts/80-live-word.md step 4 and prompts/05-setup.md question 3. lc docx undo stays the way to take a change back.
  • T10 Plain writes in the live engines: once doc-control#319 is released, the same flag through the live path, with its version pin.
  • T11 Walkthrough: on a corpus/ text: an iterative plan, one skipped part, one added aspect part, five cards (three approved, one dismissed, one refused), one undo, then a resume in a new chat. The transcript and the log give every §1 criterion.
